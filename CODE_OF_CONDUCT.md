# Code of conduct

Meridian follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and keep discussion
on the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer at ppattana@iu.edu. Reports are kept confidential, and
the maintainer may remove content or block participants who do not follow this code.
